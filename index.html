About Me

My name is Joseph Genro G. Estavillo, a 2nd-year Bachelor of Arts in Multimedia Studies student. I am 19 years old and currently live in Quezon City. To give you an impression of who I am, I'm someone who's pursuing the many things I want to do in life, such as creative work, which is why I chose BAMS.

I'm also striving to learn technical and crucial skills for the long run, like business and engineering. On the note of engineering, I plan on taking a second degree after my BAMS to gain more knowledge and widen my career path and opportunities. I wish to gain the skillset and mindset of a Creative Technologist, or of pioneers who blended art and engineering like Leonardo da Vinci.

Such a difficult task requires tons of work and passion, which is why I must work hard and improve myself every single day to fulfill my purpose, my glorious purpose.

Passion Projects

A. Senseless

Senseless is an educational, story-driven game for ages 7 and up that brings together fields like chemistry, physics, math, philosophy, and medicine. The story is set in an infinite white void, where a god-like being guides a curious Inchoate on a journey to recover lost powers and uncover the history of the world. It is designed as a casual game that players can enjoy daily on PC and mobile, and it is currently in development.

B. Edutainment YouTube Channel and Social Media Content

This project is an educational channel and social media content made to teach many people about various fields of knowledge. Creating it also lets me learn those fields myself, since I have to understand a concept well before I can explain it. It is also meant to earn money, and it is currently an idea in the planning stage.

C. Untitled Philippines Story

This is a story set in the Philippines in the 2050s to 60s, following a stranded Japanese assassin and a Filipino companion as they travel to Manila. It aims to introduce foreigners to Philippine culture while teaching topics like medicine, biology, gravity, and space through the characters' abilities. It is currently an idea in the planning stage and does not have a final title yet.
